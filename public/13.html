M-Cn That's wonderful! I guess the consumers like the new features, like the digital camera and Internet access on their phones.
W-Am Actually, our survey indicates that it's the phone's new slim design they like. It's really attractive.
M-Cn I heard Sarah Luzon is getting a nice bonus. That whole design was her idea.

  • A. A digital camera
  • B. A laptop computer
  • C. A mobile phone
  • D. A compact stereo
Explanation: The woman explicitly mentions selling "over 10,000 units of our new C-12 mobile phone model."
2. According to the survey, what led to an increase in sales?
  • A. An attractive design
  • B. A reduced price
  • C. Good advertising
  • D. New product capabilities
Explanation: The woman states that "our survey indicates that it's the phone's new slim design they like. It's really attractive."
3. What will Sarah Luzon receive?
  • A. A pay raise
  • B. An award
  • C. A promotion
  • D. A bonus
Explanation: The man mentions, "I heard Sarah Luzon is getting a nice bonus."
