## Listening Comprehension

### Questions 1-3

**[M-Am]**
Hi, Judy. It's Ted in the accounting department. I'm working on the reimbursement request from your business trip to New York last week. I've received the receipts for your travel expenses, but you forgot to attach the reimbursement form. I'll need that before we can reimburse you for your travel expenses. Would you mind sending the completed form to my office? Thanks, Judy.

#### Questions & Answers

1. **What did Judy do last week?**
   (A) She took a vacation.
   (B) She opened a bank account.
   (C) She bought office supplies.
   (D) She went on a business trip.
   **Answer: D**
> **Explanation:** Ted mentions Judy's business trip to New York last week.

2. **What is the problem?**
   (A) Seats are not available on a flight.
   (B) A form has not been received.
   (C) Some receipts are missing.
   (D) The wrong price was provided.
   **Answer: B**
> **Explanation:** Judy forgot to attach the reimbursement form.

3. **What does the speaker ask Judy to do?**
   (A) Send in a payment
   (B) Change a reservation
   (C) Return a telephone call
   (D) Submit a document
   **Answer: D**
> **Explanation:** Ted asks her to send the completed form to his office.

>
> Personalized desserts should be ordered at least one week beforehand. Customers should call the bakery to learn more about catering rates.

- **Personalized dessert:** a dessert customized for a specific occasion.
- **Catering rates:** prices for providing food for an event.

#### Key Takeaways:
1. Personalized desserts require advance ordering.
2. The bakery provides catering for corporate and private events.
3. Customers should call for catering prices.

> The first-floor lobby escalators at Canary Wharf Productions will be closed for about one week while a new motor and handrail are installed. Employees should use the stairs or passenger elevators instead of the freight elevator.

- **Freight elevator:** an elevator for equipment and heavy items.

#### Key Takeaways:
1. The escalators are closed for maintenance.
2. The closure is expected to last one week.
3. Employees should use the stairs or passenger elevators.
