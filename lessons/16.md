## Listening Comprehension

### Questions 1-3

**[Radio advertisement]**  
Do you like to learn about unusual vacation destinations? Then don't miss Radio Explorers with host Bruce Mac Williams. Each week Bruce Mac Williams gives detailed information about exotic locations, interviews leading environmentalists, and offers special tips from experienced tour guides. Radio Explorers is broadcast every Tuesday at 3 P.M., with a repeat show at 9 P.M. on Wednesday. For a complete schedule of upcoming destinations that will be discussed, visit our Website at radioexplorers.org.

#### Questions & Answers

1. **What is being advertised?**  
   (A) A travel agency  
   (B) A radio show  
   (C) A holiday tour  
   (D) A nature course  
   **Answer: B**  
> **Explanation:** The advertisement promotes Radio Explorers, a weekly broadcast.

2. **Who is Bruce Mac Williams?**  
   (A) A professor  
   (B) A tour guide  
   (C) An environmentalist  
   (D) A radio host  
   **Answer: D**  
> **Explanation:** Bruce Mac Williams is introduced as the host of Radio Explorers.

3. **What can be found on the Web site?**  
   (A) An address  
   (B) A schedule  
   (C) A price list  
   (D) An application form  
   **Answer: B**  
> **Explanation:** The speaker tells listeners to visit the Web site for a complete schedule.

### Questions 7-9

**[Automotive product advertisement]**  
People might love summer, but cars don't. During the summer, an annoying yellow dust called pollen begins appearing in the air, and everywhere else. Everyone is familiar with the thick layers of pollen that cover streets, buildings, and cars in the summer. However, many people don't realize that pollen can damage your vehicle's paint. If pollen is left on a car's paint for too long, it will leave a permanent stain. Sure, rain will wash the pollen off of a car, but you can't count on that. Use Wilkinson Brand Wax Sealer to reliably protect your car's paint from pollen. Stop by your automotive supply dealer today and ask for the brand America trusts, Wilkinson.

#### Questions & Answers

7. **What does the speaker suggest about pollen?**  
   (A) It is a common problem.  
   (B) It is unaffected by rain.  
   (C) It is unhealthy to breathe in.  
   (D) It is plentiful throughout the year.  
   **Answer: A**  
> **Explanation:** The speaker says everyone is familiar with pollen during the summer.

8. **According to the advertisement, how can pollen be harmful?**  
   (A) By blocking an air vent  
   (B) By reducing driver visibility  
   (C) By damaging car paint  
   (D) By making tires slip  
   **Answer: C**  
> **Explanation:** Pollen can damage a vehicle's paint and leave a permanent stain.

9. **What does the speaker imply when he says, "You can't count on that"?**  
   (A) Listeners should not change vacation plans.  
   (B) Listeners should not rely on the weather.  
   (C) Listeners should not trust advertisements.  
   (D) Listeners should not drive during a storm.  
   **Answer: B**  
> **Explanation:** Rain may wash pollen away, but listeners should not rely on rain to protect their cars.
