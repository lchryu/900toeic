## Listening Comprehension

### Questions 1-3

**[Airport announcement]**  
Attention all passengers waiting to board Oceana Air flight 502 to Hong Kong. Due to current weather conditions, all outgoing flights after 9 P.M. have been canceled, including the 9:30 P.M. flight to Hong Kong. The next available flight to Hong Kong will be tomorrow morning at 8:20 A.M. We will be providing hotel accommodations and shuttle bus service to those in need. Please speak to a customer service representative at the re-booking desk for further assistance. Thank you for your understanding. We sincerely apologize for this inconvenience.

#### Questions & Answers

1. **Where is this announcement most likely being made?**  
   (A) In a travel agency  
   (B) At an airport  
   (C) At a bus station  
   (D) In a hotel lobby  
   **Answer: B**  
> **Explanation:** The speaker mentions passengers, boarding, flights, and Hong Kong.

2. **What is the cause of the problem?**  
   (A) Weather conditions  
   (B) Mechanical problems  
   (C) Heavy traffic  
   (D) Absent employees  
   **Answer: A**  
> **Explanation:** The flights were canceled because of current weather conditions.

3. **What will be provided for these customers?**  
   (A) Complimentary beverages  
   (B) A partial refund  
   (C) A seating upgrade  
   (D) Hotel accommodations  
   **Answer: D**  
> **Explanation:** The announcement says hotel accommodations and shuttle bus service will be provided.
